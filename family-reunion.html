Identification & Claim Protocol

To claim or visit a rescued family member undergoing medical treatment in sealed hospital wards:

  1. Bring government Citizenship (Nagarikta) or Birth Certificate.
  2. Present registration token generated from Khoji Nepal.
  3. Police verification desk at entry gate will issue immediate visitor pass.
