About Khoji Nepal Platform

KHOJI NEPAL

Rasuwa Flood Information, Rescue & Family Tracing System

Khoji Nepal is a centralized humanitarian and public-information platform initiated in response to the devastating flash floods and landslides across Rasuwa District (Timure, Syabrubesi, Bhotekoshi, Gatlang, and Dhunche corridors).

The platform bridges critical coordination gaps between citizens searching for separated family members, disaster management authorities (Nepal Police, Nepali Army, Armed Police Force), relief distribution NGOs, and health centers.

Core Operational Directives

  • Biometric Integrity: AI and photo matching results represent "Possible Match" candidates only and never constitute definitive identity verification. Official identification is conducted through Nepal Police protocol.
  • Accessible & Lightweight: Built on pure HTML5, CSS3, and modern JavaScript to ensure ultra-fast loading on 2G/3G mobile networks in remote Himalayan corridors.
  • Verified Information Pipeline: Real-time cross-referencing with MOHA, NEOC, and local ward emergency committees to prevent misinformation.

Coordinating Partners

Ministry of Home Affairs (MOHA) District Disaster Management Committee (DDMC Rasuwa) Nepal Red Cross Society Department of Hydrology and Meteorology
